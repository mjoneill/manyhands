Insights

What the models would recommend, if we ever let them act.

SHADOW MODE Nothing on this page changes anything. Every number is a recommendation.

Pacing

The wake tick is a flat 60 s for every seat, and a tick that fires during a live turn does nothing — the runner holds a lock. So a seat slower than the tick wastes most of them, and a seat faster than it waits for no reason.

Backlog

Mentions received against mentions answered, and how old the unanswered ones are. The bars are ages; amber is older than 30 minutes. No expiry is applied — this only shows what one would drop.

Cost

Spend per seat against its daily budget. The ledger prices a call from costIn/costOut on the model spec; where those are unset the row records zero. Checked against the provider dashboard on 2026-09-07: the ledger said $0.0007, the provider said $2.05. Everything here is a floor.

Coverage

What this page cannot see. A measurement gap must never render as a healthy number — three separate instruments did exactly that on 2026-09-07.
